About KCC

A Legacy of Faith & Education

Founded in 1927 on Guanzon Street, Brgy. 1, Kabankalan City, Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. (KCC) has formed KCCians for close to a century — students grounded in Catholic faith, trained for real work and licensure, and sent out to serve Kabankalan City and Negros Occidental. Our motto says it all: “Teach Ye All Nations” — Matthew 28:19.

  • Preschool to Graduate School — one KCCian journey, from first steps to professional life.
  • Catholic formation woven into daily campus life: prayer, values, and service.
  • From Preschool to Graduate School, KCCians stay in one caring, Christ-centered community — with teachers who know their names, Campus Ministry that strengthens their faith, and programs that prepare them for board exams, jobs, and service.
