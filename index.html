College of Management, Accountancy & Technology

CMAT

Business, IT & Accountancy

BSBA • BSIT • BSA • BSAIS • BSMA • BSTM — future-ready professionals for business, technology, and tourism.

College of Education, Arts & Sciences

CEAS

Education, Arts & Sciences

BEEd • BECEd • BSEd English • BSEd Filipino • BSEd Mathematics • BSEd Science • BSEd Values Education • BPEd • ABPhilo • BSPsych — teachers, psychologists, and philosophers formed to teach, guide, and serve.

College of Criminology

CRIM

BS Criminology

Bachelor of Science in Criminology — discipline, courage, and brotherhood, preparing KCCians for the board exam and service in law enforcement and public safety.

Senior High School
