About Our Institution

A Legacy of Catholic Education in Kabankalan City

Committed to holistic human development anchored in Gospel values, academic competence, and social responsibility.

  • Christ-Centered Formation: Integrating faith, character, and ethical leadership in every discipline.
  • Accessible & Relevant Programs: Offering Basic Education, Senior High School, and Tertiary Degree Programs.
  • Community Engagement: Active outreach and service initiatives across Kabankalan City and neighboring towns.
Our Vision

A premier Catholic educational institution forming competent, compassionate, and Christ-like leaders for Church and society.

Our Mission

To deliver quality, inclusive Catholic education that nurtures intellectual excellence, moral integrity, and servant leadership.
